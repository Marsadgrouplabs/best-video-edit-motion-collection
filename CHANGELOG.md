# Changelog

## v2.0.0

First public release of **Best Video Edit & Motion Collection**.

- An always-updated collection of AI video editing and motion tools; every bundled part keeps its author's credit in `NOTICE.md`.
- Works in any language: the skill replies in the user's language, and captions follow the speaker's language.
- One full editing engine: silence removal, 9:16 reels, word-synced captions, montage, 2-camera podcast, touch studio, collage, audio clean-up, generated scenes.
- 5 motion styles: HyperFrames, onetake (non-commercial), collage B-roll, hand-drawn cartoon, 43 cinematic looks.
- Sound effects: CC0 sound kit, Openverse search with a credits file, real-sfx by Clipwing (network listening page disabled, phone pack instead), earshot by Leon Lin.
- Dubbing into another language or dialect in the speaker's own voice with ElevenLabs (workflow credited to Aly Elazab).
- `COMPONENTS.md` and `components.json` list every part with what it does, size, needs and licence. Claude offers the parts and installs all, the recommended set, or only the ones you pick; `install.ps1 -Components` and `install.sh <target> <parts>` do the same from a terminal.
